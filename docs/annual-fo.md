# Annual FO §7 supporting worksheet

The annual view prepares supporting figures for Czech personal income tax from independent activity (§7 and Annex 1). It is a review worksheet, not a personal income tax return, tax liability calculation, filing service, or social/health statement. `ready_for_filing` remains false.

## Recognition and records

- Income follows **actual receipt date** from a bank booking or a manual cash/other receipt with payment evidence. Invoice issue date and invoice status never create income. A linked bank receipt is counted once, even though its invoice also appears as a receivable.
- Issued invoices dated by 31 December show a **linked-bank balance** after linked bank movements booked by that date. This is not a fully reconciled receivable when cash or unlinked payments exist. The `nonlinked_receivables_reviewed` attestation is required when this year's manual income/refunds or unlinked bank receipts could affect receivables. Next-year receipts do not reduce the prior-year linked-bank balance. Overpaid invoices are flagged; their linked receipts are excluded from provisional income until the excess is resolved.
- Imported `archived` invoices show their issued amounts separately with **unknown payment state**. They become neither income nor presumed outstanding. For each selected year, the user can mark an archived invoice reconciled in `reviewed_archive_ids` only after checking its receipts in that year, including any classified bank or manual entries. That review does not create an income row.
- Linked CZK receipts from issued invoices use the invoice's stored VAT regime. A payer's output VAT is excluded proportionally from each partial receipt using invoice base/gross. The cumulative rounding method carries earlier receipts across tax years, so fully settled partial receipts reconcile to invoice net amount. Earlier refunds, overpayments, or manual taxable overrides block automatic allocation in the later year until reviewed. Nonpayers and identified persons use gross receipt as income. Historical invoices with unverified VAT regime and foreign-currency receipts require an explicit CZK taxable amount and evidence.
- Bank movements can be split with multiple `bank_allocations` when one payment covers activities with different percentage rates. Allocation magnitudes must add to the full transaction magnitude. An unbalanced split is displayed as unresolved and contributes nothing to provisional totals. Negative movements and unlinked positive movements require explicit classification. Refunds require a reason and explicit taxable CZK amount.
- Actual expenses require a payment date and explicit tax classification. A purchase's supply date is **not** an expense payment date. An expense bank allocation carries `taxable_czk`, a classification note, and optionally `purchase_id`; a manual paid expense carries date, `taxable_czk`, evidence, and optionally `purchase_id`. Multiple payments may reference the same purchase. An unlinked purchase issued in the selected year is flagged until linked to a paid expense or deliberately marked reviewed. Expense amounts are asserted by the user, including treatment of any input VAT; the module does not infer deductible VAT from purchase totals.
- Manual entries and foreign-currency allocations require explicit CZK value; no exchange rate is guessed. Manual entries require evidence and foreign-currency conversion note. The user must reconcile independent manual entries against bank imports to avoid duplicates.

## Expense methods and scope

Per-year config chooses `percentage` or `actual`. No method is chosen by default. For percentage expenses, income needs an explicit §7 activity category (`trade_80`, `trade_60`, `other_40`, `rent_30`), either as a default for linked receipts or on individual allocations. Each category uses the published cap: 80% / 1,600,000 Kč, 60% / 1,200,000 Kč, 40% / 800,000 Kč, 30% / 600,000 Kč. The categories are user assertions; the app cannot decide whether an activity is a craft trade, ordinary trade, other independent activity, or rental of business assets. Actual-expense mode does not require percentage categories.

The rates and caps have been checked for 2025–2026 only (checked 2026-10-03). Other years can show supporting rows but carry `year_rules_unverified` and cannot be complete. The current/open year is always provisional. The app does not infer personal tax credits, deductions, other income sections, loss adjustments, final tax, health or social premiums, or filing eligibility.

`complete` means no worksheet issues, user confirmation is current, and the year has ended. The explicit `confirm_records_complete` action stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of source invoices, bank transactions, purchases, other metadata (including import history), the year's classifications, and earlier years' classifications that can affect cross-year receipts. A later edit invalidates the confirmation. Earlier years' confirmation hashes are omitted so confirming an unchanged earlier worksheet alone does not invalidate a later year. This is a data completeness check for the worksheet, not a certification of tax filing readiness.

The CSV begins with its worksheet label, year, method, figures, category caps, completeness status, and all unresolved issue codes/messages. It then includes traceable receipt, expense, receivable, and archived invoice rows. It can be exported when incomplete. Text fields starting with spreadsheet formula characters are escaped.

## Backups and API

Year config is stored in `meta` under `annual_fo_config_<year>`, included in existing JSON backup/restore. The dispatch actions are `annual.config`, `annual.configure`, `annual.summary`, and `annual.csv`; each takes `year`, and configure also takes the full `config` object. The module exposes `annual_config`, `annual_save_config`, `annual_summary`, and `annual_csv` for dispatch.

The finance administration describes §7 income, tax evidence, and percentage expense categories/caps on its [OSVČ guidance page](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-prijmu/fyzicke-osoby/podnikatel-osvc). It also publishes [2025 personal income tax guidance](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-prijmu/dotazy-a-odpovedi/dan-z-prijmu-fyzickych-osob/aktualne-k-dani-z-prijmu-fyzickych-osob-2025) and the [Annex 1 form listing](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/danove-tiskopisy?rok=2025).
